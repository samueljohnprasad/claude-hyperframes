# The five-ticket dossier

A small cinema ticket leads through five paper worlds. We open on an oversized five already visible, then enter each film through its title: a bench and feather, a jury table, a life-saving ledger, prison bars opening onto hope, and a family under puppet strings. Each miniature stages the reason to watch the film as the narrator says it. The ticket fragments form a watchlist at the end.

This uses the linked style's material and motion grammar, with original layouts and film-specific illustrations. It does not reproduce its demo's chase or any movie's title design. The countdown is explicitly an editorial selection.

The narration controls timing. Foley marks paper arriving and major cuts; it remains secondary to speech. Music recedes further during Schindler's List.
