Number three: Schindler's List. A businessman saves Jewish lives during the Holocaust. Devastating, humane, and essential.

## shawshank (33.05 s)

Number two: The Shawshank Redemption. Friendship and hope survive decades behind bars. Its emotional payoff makes the journey worth it.

## godfather (42.80 s)

Number one: The Godfather. Michael Corleone's transformation turns a crime story into a tragedy about family, loyalty, and power.
