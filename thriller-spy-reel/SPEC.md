# Five Hollywood Dramas

GOAL: Give a movie fan five memorable drama recommendations and a reason to watch each.
OUTPUT: HyperFrames, 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, narrated English Instagram reel, approximately 55 seconds.
STORY: An editorial countdown, not an objective ranking. Each title becomes a paper-cut stage for its central conflict. A small mustard ticket travels between the five worlds and becomes the saved watchlist.
REFERENCE: https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/blob/main/styles/spy-titles/STYLE.md
LOOK: Four inks: #1b1714, #efe4c9, #d23a22, #e2a52a. No gradients. Fixed scissor edges, sampled at 9 px with 1.8 px displacement; paper shadows at 3 px / 4 px, 5 px blur, 0.28 opacity. Fixed fibre texture. Condensed recut title lettering, geometric sans captions at 44 px. Figures are anonymous story silhouettes, not a visible narrator.
SAFE AREA: All essential words within x=108..920 and y=185..1320. Decorative paper may bleed to the edges. Captions use one or two lines on a cut strip, below the illustration.
MOTION: Voice is the clock. Cuts occur at line starts; titles arrive on spoken names. Figures on twos (12 fps); camera and paper strips at 24 fps. Entrances 0.18–0.35 s, power3.out; no dissolves. Each scene has a second action timed to its explanation.
SHOTS:
1. Hook: five ticket corners slice into a giant 5; title is readable on frame zero.
2. #5 Forrest Gump (1994): bench made from title-like paper bars, seated silhouette, feather drifting into a ticket.
3. #4 12 Angry Men (1957): twelve angular jurors around a long diagonal table; one dissenting figure rises.
4. #3 Schindler's List (1993): cream ledger on black, names represented by quiet ruled marks, red coat silhouette; restrained motion and reduced music.
5. #2 The Shawshank Redemption (1994): tall title pillars become prison bars; the central figure steps toward an opening and raises both arms.
6. #1 The Godfather (1972): family silhouettes inside oversized lettering, red puppet strings tighten around the central figure.
7. Payoff: five ticket pieces assemble into a saveable watchlist. Ask which drama should be first.
AUDIO: Local Kokoro bm_george, speed 1.04; phrase and word timestamps determine all cuts and captions. Licensed recorded music and paper Foley only. Target -16 LUFS, master true peak <= -4.5 dBTP. SFX under speech at least 10 dB below local voice RMS, music at least 17 dB below. Export stems and mix measurements.
BANNED: Film-poster imitations, real movie logos, footage placeholders, gradients, glows, particles, halftone, crawling edges, repetitive whooshes, drifting titles, bullet-slide scenes, a new presenter design, changing existing horror-reel files.
QA: First frame, midpoint of every scene, each caption boundary, both sides of every cut, final hold; final AAC loudness, peak, mono, duration, and per-cue voice/SFX hierarchy.
